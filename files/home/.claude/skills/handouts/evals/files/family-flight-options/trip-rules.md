# Winter 2026 family trip: flight search rules

All six options in `flight-options-2026-10-01.json` already satisfy every rule below. Prices were quoted on 2026-10-01 and are not held.

## Must

- Four travelers: two adults, children aged 2 and 5, each with their own economy seat. Budget two checked bags for the whole party on each ticket; a zero bags or seats line means these are included in the fare.
- Route: Singapore → Boston → Grand Junction, Colorado → Singapore, in that order.
- Christmas Day is a full, non-travel day in Boston.
- At least six full, non-travel days in Boston and at least six in Colorado. Arrival and departure dates do not count as full days.
- At least 150 minutes to connect after first US entry; at least 90 minutes at other connections, except a direct flight-to-rental-car pickup.
- Denver ↔ Grand Junction drives count as four hours of travel.

## Preferences

- Fewest extra workdays. Workdays are weekdays outside December 24 – January 1.
- Lower all-in cost: fares plus bags, seat selection, and the rental car for any Denver drive.
- Flying all the way into Grand Junction is worth up to about $800 over driving from Denver.
- A single ticket per journey is nicer, but separate tickets are fine when the risk is spelled out.
- Early morning departure on the Boston → Colorado day.
